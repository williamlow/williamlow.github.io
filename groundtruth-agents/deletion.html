Groundtruth Agent Prototype Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: 19 May 2026

This page explains how testers can request deletion of data associated with the Groundtruth Agent Prototype.

What data may be deleted

If you have sent messages to the prototype WhatsApp number, the prototype may hold data such as:

  • Your WhatsApp phone number or a hashed version of it;
  • Message text you sent to the prototype;
  • WhatsApp message IDs, timestamps, and delivery metadata;
  • Draft incident records created from your messages;
  • Development logs containing webhook payloads.

How to request deletion

To request deletion of your data, send an email to: william.d.low@gmail.com

Please include:

  1. The WhatsApp phone number you used to message the prototype;
  2. The approximate date or dates when you sent messages;
  3. A short statement saying that you want your prototype data deleted.

Example request:

“Please delete any data associated with my WhatsApp messages to the Groundtruth Agent Prototype. My WhatsApp number is [your number], and I used the prototype around [date].”

What happens next

After receiving a deletion request, the prototype operator will search for matching records and delete or anonymise associated prototype data where reasonably possible.

Limits

Some data may already have been deleted during routine development cleanup. Some limited technical records may be retained temporarily where needed for security, debugging, legal, or audit reasons.

Contact

For deletion requests or questions, contact: william.d.low@gmail.com

These instructions apply only to the Groundtruth Agent Prototype test environment.
