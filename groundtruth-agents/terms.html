Groundtruth Agent Prototype Terms of Service

Last updated: 19 May 2026

This is a limited private prototype for testing WhatsApp-based incident-reporting workflows. It is not a public emergency service, public reporting mechanism, or official alerting system.

1. Purpose of the prototype

The Groundtruth Agent Prototype is a test system designed to explore whether WhatsApp messages can be converted into structured draft incident records and followed up with clarifying questions.

The prototype is for controlled testing only. It should not be relied on for emergency response, life-safety decisions, legal decisions, operational deployment, or public information.

2. Who may use the prototype

The prototype is intended only for authorised testers. Do not use the prototype unless you have been invited to participate in testing.

3. Appropriate use

When using the prototype, you agree not to:

  • Send information that could put you or another person at risk;
  • Send names, phone numbers, addresses, or identifying details of affected people unless essential for an authorised test;
  • Send false, misleading, unlawful, abusive, or harmful content;
  • Use the prototype for emergency reporting or urgent requests for assistance;
  • Attempt to interfere with, overload, reverse engineer, or misuse the system.

4. Safety notice

Only share information that is safe for you and others to share. Do not include sensitive personal details, precise household locations, or information that could expose individuals or communities to harm.

5. AI-generated outputs

The prototype may use artificial intelligence to classify messages, extract structured information, and draft follow-up questions. AI-generated outputs may be incomplete, inaccurate, or misleading.

Any structured records produced by the prototype should be treated as draft test outputs requiring human review.

6. No emergency or professional advice

The prototype does not provide emergency assistance, medical advice, security advice, legal advice, or humanitarian response services. If you need urgent help, use appropriate official or trusted emergency and support channels.

7. Data and privacy

Use of the prototype is subject to the Groundtruth Agent Prototype Privacy Policy. The system may process WhatsApp message content, phone numbers, message metadata, and test records as described in that policy.

8. Availability and changes

The prototype may be changed, suspended, or discontinued at any time without notice. Features may not work as expected during testing.

9. Limitation of liability

The prototype is provided for testing on an “as is” and “as available” basis. To the maximum extent permitted by law, the operators of the prototype are not liable for loss, harm, or damage arising from use of, inability to use, or reliance on the prototype.
