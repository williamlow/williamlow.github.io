Groundtruth Agent Prototype Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 May 2026

This is a limited private prototype for testing WhatsApp-based incident-reporting workflows. It is not a public emergency reporting service.

Purpose

The Groundtruth Agent Prototype tests a workflow for receiving short incident reports through WhatsApp, converting them into structured draft records, and asking follow-up questions where important information is missing.

Information collected

If you send a message to the prototype WhatsApp number, the system may process:

  • Your WhatsApp phone number;
  • The text of messages you send;
  • Message metadata provided by WhatsApp, such as message IDs, timestamps, and delivery status;
  • Any information you voluntarily include in your message.

Testers should not send sensitive personal information, names of affected individuals, precise household locations, or information that could put anyone at risk.

How information is used

Information is used only to test and improve the prototype workflow, including:

  • Receiving WhatsApp messages;
  • Extracting structured incident information;
  • Identifying missing information;
  • Sending follow-up questions;
  • Debugging and improving the system.

AI processing

Messages may be processed using an AI model to extract structured information and draft follow-up questions. The system may produce imperfect outputs, and all outputs should be treated as draft information for testing only.

Data storage

Messages and webhook payloads may be stored in development logs or a prototype database. Access is limited to the prototype developer or authorised testers.

Data sharing

Information is not sold. Information is not shared publicly. Information may be processed by service providers used to operate the prototype, such as hosting, messaging, database, and AI API providers.

Data retention

Test data may be deleted periodically during development. Testers can request deletion of messages they have sent to the prototype.

Contact

This policy applies only to the Groundtruth Agent Prototype test environment.
